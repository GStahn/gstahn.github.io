**Context:** I use this meme in an exercise on *time-inconsistent decision-making* in behavioral economics. It clearly illustrates how individuals who heavily discount their future utility and who fail to predict their own later decisions are potentially worse off than *sophisticated decision-makers*, who realistically anticipate their future (lack of) self-control.
